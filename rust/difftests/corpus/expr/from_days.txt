## FROM_DAYS: the inverse of TO_DAYS (days_from_civil's inverse,
## civil_from_days, offset by TO_DAYS's own epoch constant); outside the
## valid range (year 0001 through 9999) real TiDB returns the "zero date"
## string; a narrow, clearly-anomalous NULL sub-band immediately above the
## valid range is deliberately out of scope (see date_fn::from_days's doc
## comment) and not tested here. Scoped to Int arguments only (a string or
## decimal argument would need MySQL's general numeric coercion, which this
## codebase doesn't otherwise model).
