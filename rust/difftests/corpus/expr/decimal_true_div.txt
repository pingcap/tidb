## `/` (true division): always promotes both operands to Decimal, even for
## two Int operands. Result scale is the DIVIDEND's own scale plus 4 (MySQL's
## div_precision_increment) — the divisor's own scale never affects it.
## Rounds (not truncates); division by zero is NULL; sign follows XOR.
