## ROUND(x)/ROUND(x, d)/TRUNCATE(x, d): NULL if any argument is NULL.
## Int stays Int for 1-arg ROUND (a plain passthrough); the 2-arg forms
## round-trip through f64 exactly like real TiDB does. Decimal NEVER
## collapses to Int (unlike CEIL/FLOOR -- ROUND(3.14159) is DEC:3, not
## INT:3) and rounds ties AWAY from zero, clamped to DECIMAL's max scale
## (30) for a positive d. Float rounds ties TO EVEN -- the OPPOSITE
## tie-breaking rule from Decimal -- via Go's exact types.Round/
## types.Truncate algorithm (multiply by 10^d, round/truncate, divide
## back), including Go's own math.Pow10 lookup table (NOT a mathematically
## "more correct" power of ten -- confirmed the two disagree by 1 ULP for
## most exponents by dumping bit patterns, not assumed). Every case here
## was confirmed via goeval, then cross-checked against
## pkg/expression/builtin_math.go and pkg/types/helper.go.
