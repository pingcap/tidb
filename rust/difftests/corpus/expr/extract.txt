## `EXTRACT(unit FROM expr)` -- a genuinely separate general-purpose
## extraction syntax from `INTERVAL value unit`, with its own grammar
## (unit first, then FROM, then value) and restore form. Sugar for
## calling the SAME single-argument function `unit` already names --
## every simple unit this project's evaluator already supports as a
## standalone function (YEAR/MONTH/DAY/QUARTER/HOUR/MINUTE/SECOND) works
## identically through EXTRACT. NULL propagates; the unit keyword is
## case-insensitive. Compound units (DAY_HOUR, YEAR_MONTH, ...) and WEEK
## are real MySQL/TiDB grammar but deliberately not modelled here --
## excluded from this corpus since real TiDB gives them real values this
## project intentionally does not reproduce.
extract(year from '2024-03-15')
extract(month from '2024-03-15')
extract(day from '2024-03-15')
extract(quarter from '2024-03-15')
extract(hour from '2024-03-15 10:30:45')
extract(minute from '2024-03-15 10:30:45')
extract(second from '2024-03-15 10:30:45')
extract(year from NULL)
extract(YEAR from '2024-03-15')
extract(hour from '25:30:45')
