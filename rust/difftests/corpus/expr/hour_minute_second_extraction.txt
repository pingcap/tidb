## HOUR/MINUTE/SECOND: real TiDB's own two-path algorithm (confirmed via
## goeval, not assumed), depending on whether the argument contains a ':'.
## WITH a colon: an optional "[DATE ]" prefix (a JUNK prefix invalidates
## the whole value, not just gets ignored) followed by H:M[:S] (S defaults
## to 0); H may exceed 23 (TiDB's TIME domain is elapsed-time, not
## wall-clock), clamped -- along with M and S -- to exactly 838:59:59 once
## H exceeds 838, even when M/S were individually valid; M/S must each be
## 0-59 or the WHOLE value is NULL regardless of H. WITHOUT a colon
## (including a bare DATE-only value, which is NOT (0,0,0), a genuinely
## surprising real behavior): the value's own leading digit run decodes as
## a right-aligned HHMMSS number (SECOND = N%100, MINUTE = (N/100)%100,
## HOUR = N/10000), the same rule an integer literal already uses.
