## `+`/`-`/`*`/`/`/`MOD` over Float: an Int or Decimal operand promotes to
## `f64` (Float dominates Decimal, the OPPOSITE of how Decimal dominates
## Int); NATIVE f64 arithmetic throughout, since Rust's f64 already
## matches Go's IEEE-754 semantics bit for bit (confirmed, not assumed);
## division/MOD by zero is NULL, same as Int/Decimal; underflow to zero is
## fine, overflow to +/-infinity is a genuine evaluation error (not
## covered here — ERR goldens aren't asserted by the differential harness,
## covered instead by a direct unit-test assertion)
