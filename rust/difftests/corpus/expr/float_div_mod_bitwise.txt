## `DIV` truncates its quotient toward zero (an Int result), same as
## Int/Decimal; `MOD` keeps native f64 remainder semantics (so a
## fractional result carries the same floating-point rounding noise real
## MySQL's own f64 does, e.g. 100.7 mod 3 != exactly 1.7); bitwise/shift
## operators round to the nearest i64 first -- but TIES TO EVEN, the
## OPPOSITE tie-breaking rule from Decimal's own bitwise conversion (ties
## away from zero) -- a real, easy-to-miss asymmetry confirmed via
## goeval, not assumed: ~2.5 is -3 (2.5 rounds to the even 2), not -4
