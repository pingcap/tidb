## [[NOT] ENFORCED]`, defaulting to ENFORCED when neither is written; a
## `CONSTRAINT` with no name behaves as if absent, same as PRIMARY KEY/
## UNIQUE. Also covers table-level constraint ORDERING: PRIMARY KEY/UNIQUE/

## canonical order (a real, previously-unverified rule this corpus locks in)
