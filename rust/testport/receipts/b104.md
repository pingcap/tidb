## Coverage notes

`drop_tables` ports the synchronous portion of Go's `TestDropTables` through
`run_drop_table_in`: missing tables and missing qualified schemas return
`ErrBadTable` (1051), `IF EXISTS` removes existing names while reporting the
missing name, and non-`IF EXISTS` multi-table DROP removes existing names
before returning the missing-table error. Both table-name orderings are
covered.

The ignored mappings preserve the exact Go source file, line, and declaration
in their doc comments. They are documentary receipts, not weaker substitute
assertions. The missing carriers include, among others:

- online DDL state transitions and DML injected through failpoint hooks;
- DDL job persistence, history, retries, cancellation, and scheduler locks;
- session time-zone, warning, transaction, savepoint, metadata-lock, and
  information-schema behavior;
- TiKV disk-full, PD/domain schema-version, GC, and cluster-service behavior;
- private Go helpers such as `getIntervalFromPolicy`, `isIgnorableSpec`,
  `checkDuplicateConstraint`, and `getTableDataKeyRanges`.
