## Structural facts that shaped the ports

- 54 of the 60 Go tests drive Go's ONLINE schema-change state machine: a
  failpoint hook (`afterWaitSchemaSynced` / `beforeRunOneJobStep`) holds a
  DDL job in `StateDeleteOnly`/`StateWriteOnly`/`StateWriteReorganization`/
  `StateDeleteReorganization` while DML runs against the intermediate schema
  (`runTestInSchemaState`, `pkg/ddl/db_change_test.go:792`). This crate
  applies DDL synchronously — no job queue, no intermediate states, no
  failpoints (documented in `crate::ddl`'s module header) — so those tests
  are recorded as `ignored-gap`, each keeping its Go recipe in the Rust doc
  comment for whoever closes the gap.
- `pkg/ddl/copr` already had three of its four master tests ported inline in
  `ddl_copr.rs::tests` (the file predates the master addition of
  `TestCopContextConditionUsesFixedCollation`); the three were re-verified
  against the master bodies this batch and the fourth is recorded as a gap
  (no `BuildSimpleExpr` hook exists to observe the fixed collation flag).
- The cached-table admission rules of `(*executor).AlterTableCache`
  (`pkg/ddl/executor.go:6940`) — idempotence (`:6946`), temporary refusal
  with the exact argument `"alter temporary table cache"` (`:6954`),
  `"partition mode"` (`:6958`), and `"table too large"` (`:6966`) — ARE
  modeled by this crate (`ddl/table_cache.rs::alter_cache_action`,
  `kv_table/cache.rs::enable_cache`), so `TestAlterTableCache` and
  `TestCacheTableSizeLimit` are ported for real at the metadata level.
- `TestAlterIndexVisibility` is ported at the metadata level: Go reads
  `information_schema.tidb_indexes.is_visible`, which is the session layer's
  projection of the `KvIndex.visible` flag this crate owns and
  `alter_metadata::alter_index_visibility_action` mutates.
