## Gaps

- go-parity-gap: Go `HostedEmbedding` section (config.go ~line 339/1082) and
  its "hosted-embedding can only be configured for starter deploy mode"
  Load/Valid checks are not transcreated; test is `#[ignore]`.
- Go TestConfig's tracing (`OpenTracing.ToTracingConfig`) and TLS
  (`ClusterSecurity.ToTLSConfig`) assertions target jaeger/rustls integration
  that the crate does not model; those specific asserts were not ported.
