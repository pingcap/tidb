## Rust follow-up: Go-shaped error constructors

The complete five-artifact root inventory remains authoritative: `BUILD.bazel`,
`terror.go`, `ddl_terror.go`, `terror_test.go`, and `main_test.go` were read in
full before this edit. The nested `exeerrors` and `plannererrors` packages are
separate atomic surfaces. In the Rust owner, `ErrClass::new_std` and
`ErrClass::new_std_err` are direct counterparts of Go's `ErrClass.NewStd` and
`NewStdErr`; `new_plain_err` remains crate-private implementation machinery.

The two public Rust constructors no longer carry Rust-only `#[must_use]`
diagnostics, allowing callers to discard their returned error objects as Go
callers can. The focused in-module regression constructs both forms and
discards their returns under `#[deny(unused_must_use)]`.

Pre-fix proof: the detached pre-fix owner failed to compile the regression with
exactly two diagnostics, one for each constructor. After removing the
annotations, the focused test and all five dbterror tests pass.
