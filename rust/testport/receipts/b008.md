## Notes / not verified locally

- `PROTOC` env var was required locally (system protoc absent); CI must
  provide protoc as it already does for other crates.
- `Job.String()` start-time fragment renders through the process-local zone
  exactly like Go, so that segment is pinned by stable prefix/suffix rather
  than an exact string.
- Two pre-existing `unused_mut` warnings in `tidb-model/src/placement.rs`
  were observed while compiling the dependency; they exist on the clean tree
  and were not touched.
