with real assertions:

  before trusting a bucket repeat

  falling through to the uniform average otherwise.

  `rust/crates/tidb-planner/src/cardinality/row_count_estimator.rs:357`
  now requires `hist_count > 0` before trusting a matched bucket repeat,
  matching Go's uniform fallback.
